Build specification: F0–F8

A gate = a list of conditions at the end of a phase. Until all of them hold, the next phase does not begin.

F0 · design on paperA closed list of scenarios, the model/code split, a measurable “done”.
F1 · verify the coreThe riskiest step measured against a real sample — accuracy, cost and time as numbers. A short, synthetic sample lies systematically in your favour.
F2 · skeleton and contractsModules run on their own; the test environment physically has no outbound channels.
F3 · deterministic backboneThe process passes with manual input, still without a model. Only two endings: it failed and you know, or it went well.
F4 · model and evalsThe model does only its three jobs. Evals in CI, the tool recall/precision and parameter accuracy metrics. Hostile input cannot push the agent off-scenario.
F5 · gates and limitsThe mode follows the reversibility of the mistake, not trust. Identity from the channel. Least possible power for tools. Autonomy grows over time and can be taken back.
F6 · failure and kill switchA report goes to a person, not just to a log. The kill switch is one action, tested.
F7 · deploymentSee the running version, know the cost, check by hand for the first week. Error vs. variance: 3–5 runs, an 80 % threshold.
F8 · operation and growthEvery error and every successful pass goes into the evals. Watch distribution shift, not just the error rate.
The minimum that is never skipped, even for the smallest agent: F1 a real sample · F3 two outcomes · F6 a kill switch. These are the three whose missing versions are recognised only from the damage.

First live application: JobWatch

The specification was applied to JobWatch — a daily agent running live on Cloudflare. The result is a record that the specification works: it found three defects that the tests had not, because it asked different questions than tests do.

Kill switchThe Stop button closed the run record, but the pipeline read no stop flag and carried on. F6
Silent crashThe exception ended up in the log; notifications were sent only on findings, so “found nothing” and “crashed” looked identical from outside. F6
Foreign textListing text written by other people went into the model unwrapped. F4

The full analysis, including what held up: 02-pripady/AUDIT-job-watch.en.md.

Where the methodology comes from

Interview with Marek BartošThe Keci a politika podcast, the special “Artificial intelligence is our UFO”. The source of the principle “AI recognises, code executes”, of the gates, the cross-check and the economics of the process. A map of 19 timestamps → chapters in 00-zdroje/ZDROJE.en.md.
Michael AlbadaBuilding Applications with AI Agents (O'Reilly 2025). Contributed the measurement metrics, telling an error from variance, the growth of autonomy and the four ways human oversight fails.
Public specificationsAnthropic Building effective agents (workflow vs. agent, five patterns) · 12-Factor Agents (own your loop, your context and your prompts).
Bartoš's approach has a name in the public taxonomy: by Anthropic's terms it is a workflow, not an agent — a model and tools driven by code written in advance. That is, the category Anthropic itself recommends as the first choice, because it is predictable.
